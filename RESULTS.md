# FedXCrop results

Every number below was produced by code in this repository and is traceable to a
file under `results/`. The file is named next to each table. Nothing here is
transcribed by hand.

Generated by `scripts/make_report.py` from 27 finished runs.

## Protocol

- Split: one fixed stratified 80/10/10 split at seed 42, built once and committed.
  Train 43,447, validation 5,430, test 5,428 images over 38 classes.
- Federated runs start every client and the global model from ImageNet weights.
  Clients hold disjoint shards of the full train split. Validation and test are
  global and are never partitioned.
- The round or epoch carried to test is chosen by validation accuracy alone. The
  test split is read once per run.
- Reported rates are reproducible from the integer counts saved with them.

## What is and is not statistically significant

McNemar's test on the same 5428 test images, seed 0.

Significant at the 0.05 level:

- fedprox alpha=0.1 mu=0.1 vs fedavg alpha=0.1: 33 images only the first got right against 70 the other way, p = 0.000389
- fedavg IID vs centralized: 8 images only the first got right against 32 the other way, p = 0.000276
- fedavg alpha=0.1 vs fedavg IID: 28 images only the first got right against 72 the other way, p = 1.71e-05

Not significant, meaning the data does not separate these configurations:

- fedprox alpha=0.1 mu=0.001 vs fedavg alpha=0.1: 38 against 35 discordant images, p = 0.815
- fedprox alpha=0.1 mu=0.01 vs fedavg alpha=0.1: 37 against 46 discordant images, p = 0.38
- fedprox alpha=0.5 mu=0.001 vs fedavg alpha=0.5: 18 against 17 discordant images, p = 1
- fedprox alpha=1 mu=0.001 vs fedavg alpha=1: 13 against 19 discordant images, p = 0.377
- fedavg alpha=0.5 vs fedavg IID: 28 against 42 discordant images, p = 0.12
- fedavg alpha=1 vs fedavg IID: 21 against 19 discordant images, p = 0.874

## Legacy protocol comparison

The original protocol initialized every client from the centralized model that
had already seen the whole training set, and distributed only a small subset of
the training data. Both are reproduced here so the corrected numbers can be read
against them.

- fedavg_dirichlet_alpha0.5_K5_legacyinit_seed0: test accuracy 98.91 (5369/5428), macro F1 98.34, after 5.0 rounds

Under the corrected protocol the same strategy reaches fedavg alpha=0.1 97.78, fedavg alpha=0.5 98.59, fedavg alpha=1 98.81, fedavg IID 98.81.

## Near duplicate leakage

- 157 of 5428 test images (2.89 percent) have a perceptual hash match within Hamming distance 4 in the train split.
- 122 of those share the label, which is the kind that inflates accuracy; 35 do not.
- 3 test images have an identical hash to a train image.
- Most affected classes: Raspberry___healthy (22 percent), Soybean___healthy (20 percent), Cherry_(including_sour)___healthy (8 percent).

The split was not modified, so these numbers stay comparable with published
PlantVillage results, which are subject to the same overlap. It does mean the
absolute accuracies here, and in the literature, are optimistic.

Source: `results/data/leakage_report.json`

## Limitations

- PlantVillage images are single leaves photographed against a uniform background
  in a lab. Nothing here establishes field performance, and no out of distribution
  test set was used.
- Clients are simulated by partitioning one dataset. No data was held by separate
  parties and no network was involved.
- No formal privacy mechanism is used: no differential privacy and no secure
  aggregation. Transmitting weights rather than images is not by itself a privacy
  guarantee, since model updates can leak training data.
- Lesion masks are derived from a colour rule, not annotated. They under-detect dark
  necrotic spots and flag whole leaves for chlorotic diseases, so lesion localization
  numbers are weaker evidence than the leaf energy ratio, whose mask comes from the
  dataset itself. Per class coverage is in
  `results/xai/lesion_mask_coverage_by_class.csv`.
- Train and test share near duplicate photographs of the same physical leaves, as
  quantified above.
- Accuracy on this dataset sits near a ceiling for every configuration, so the
  differences under study are small and require the intervals and paired tests
  reported here to interpret.
